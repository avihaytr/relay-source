# Relay branding policy

**ADOPTED — Version 1.0. Effective date: 2026-11-18.** The owner explicitly approved this policy. Branding permissions below apply to the extent the Relay project holds the relevant rights; the separate assets remain outside MPL-2.0. Adoption is not an announcement of a public repository or binary release and does not establish independently verified third-party clearance.

## Scope and relationship to source licenses

This policy covers use of the Relay application name as a product identifier and the current monitor/phone/arrow logo and app icons listed in [the branding manifest](licenses/BRANDING_ASSET_MANIFEST.json). The name is not an assertion of ownership of the ordinary word “relay.” The policy does not cover fonts, third-party material, unrelated artwork, or the procedural drawing code already licensed in `src/view.rs`.

Approved source and explicitly scoped test data remain under MPL-2.0. Modified forks may freely use and redistribute MPL-covered code according to that license. Nothing here restricts using, modifying, forking, selling or redistributing that code under its applicable license. A source-code license does not automatically grant branding rights. These branding terms are separate and are not an OSI-approved software license or a claim that all repository contents are MPL-covered.

## Permissions

To the extent the Relay project can grant the relevant rights, the permission is worldwide, non-exclusive and royalty-free, for personal and commercial use:

- Copy and redistribute the unmodified branding with genuine Relay source distributions and builds. A genuine build is compiled from an identified, unmodified Relay source version, allowing ordinary build configuration, dependency selection and packaging metadata changes that do not misrepresent application behavior or origin. An independently distributed unmodified build may retain the branding under these terms, but must identify its distributor and must not imply official release status, endorsement or support merely because it uses the original branding.
- Make technical renditions needed for that use, such as proportional resizing, rasterization and platform icon packaging, without changing the recognizable design. Preserve this policy and the applicable source/third-party notices with distributions.
- Redistribute source forks retaining the original branding assets for source completeness, attribution and reproducibility, without implying endorsement, project approval, support or official status. Keeping these files in a source fork does not authorize using them as the product identity of a distributed modified application.
- Independently distributed modified application builds must use a distinguishable product name **and** icon unless separately authorized by the Relay project to use its branding. Merely adding an “Unofficial” label does not create an exception. This requirement concerns the separately licensed product identity, not the right to modify or redistribute MPL-covered code. Truthful attribution such as “based on Relay” remains permitted without implying endorsement.
- Descriptive derivative names such as “Relay-Python” are permitted for unofficial forks, provided they use distinct visual branding and clearly identify themselves as independent projects. Such a name satisfies the distinguishable-product-name requirement above when the overall presentation distinguishes the fork from Relay; it does not imply authorization, endorsement or official status.
- Forks may use independently created branding inspired by Relay's general design concepts, provided it is sufficiently distinguishable from the official Relay branding and does not imply endorsement or official status. This allowance does not authorize copying or modifying Relay's separate branding assets beyond the permissions above and does not restrict rights in MPL-covered code.
- Use the name and accurate screenshots/logo reproductions to identify, document, review or discuss Relay truthfully, without implying endorsement.

No permission is granted to market a different product as the official Relay application, claim endorsement without agreement, or publish a redesigned logo as an official Relay mark. Other creative logo modifications or uses outside this policy require separate permission where relevant rights exist. No requirement here overrides rights already available under applicable law or limits rights in MPL-covered code.

## Rights and limitations

The documented workflow is one owner-provided reference depicting the previous Relay design, ChatGPT generation of a new design, an icon-only/background-removal refinement, then Codex SVG conversion and application renditions. The scope of protectable human authorship, if any, and the reference's independent rights have not been conclusively verified. AI generation and vectorization do not themselves establish exclusive copyright ownership or third-party clearance.

This policy asserts no trademark registration and makes no representation of exclusive copyright, worldwide trademark priority, freedom from similar names, or cleared third-party references. It grants only rights the grantor actually holds, if any; it cannot grant third-party rights. No warranty of title, non-infringement or fitness is offered to the extent applicable law permits. Trademark, consumer-protection and other applicable law remain relevant regardless of copyright status.
